How This Book Aligns with CAS-005

This page summarizes the primary CompTIA SecurityX (CAS-005) objective alignment for each chapter in the book. The intent is to make the chapter-to-exam mapping easy to scan in one place without crowding the opening pages of every chapter.

The table below highlights the main objectives each chapter was written to cover. Some objectives echo across multiple chapters, but the listed codes identify the primary home for that content.

Chapter-to-Objective Map

Chapter 2: Risk Management and Threat Modeling 1.2, 1.4 Risk assessment, threat modeling, BCDR, and third-party risk
Chapter 3: Navigating Emerging Threats: AI and the Evolving Landscape 1.5 AI governance, AI misuse, and model-specific security risks

Domain Coverage At A Glance

  • Domain 1: Governance, Risk, and Compliance is concentrated in Chapters 1-3, then reinforced in Chapters 7, 9, and 10 where compliance and governance decisions shape architecture and operations.
  • Domain 2: Security Architecture is centered in Chapters 4-8, with identity, cloud, and cryptography treated as architectural design problems rather than isolated tools.
  • Domain 3: Security Engineering is concentrated in Chapters 5-10, where the focus shifts to troubleshooting and implementing controls across IAM, hardware, endpoints, networks, OT, and crypto.
  • Domain 4: Security Operations is centered in Chapters 11-12, where monitoring, hunting, vulnerability management, automation, and incident response come together.

How To Use This Page

  • Use it as a study-planning map if you want to review the book by CAS-005 objective rather than by chapter order.
  • Use the chapter links above if you want to jump directly from an exam domain to the chapter that carries the most depth on that topic.
  • Treat the PBQ at the end of each chapter as the applied companion to that chapter’s objective coverage.
